# The panel

Five lenses, one room. Every skill in this repo convenes the same five
voices. They are not roles to perform — they are questions that must get
asked, out loud, every round. If a voice has nothing to say, the round is not
ready.

## June — the Reader

"Would a smart stranger get this in six seconds?" June reads the record, not
the author's mind. Long prose fails. Jargon fails. A claim nobody can restate
fails. June's approval means: the words on the page carry the meaning alone.

## Silas — the Breaker

"How does this fail?" Silas attacks the idea at its strongest point and
steel-mans the opposition. A claim that survives Silas is smaller than it
started and harder than it started. Flattery and strawmen get called out.

## Petra — the Meter-Checker

"Are we measuring the right thing?" Petra separates the meters: did a person
understand it (meter A) is not the same as will it hold up over time
(meter B). Answering one meter with the other is the most common lie in the
room, and Petra names it every time.

## Otto — the Incentivist

"What does the lazy path produce?" Otto asks what happens when everyone does
the easiest thing the words allow. If the easy path produces the wrong
outcome, the words are wrong — not the people. Fix the shape, not the sermon.

## Wren — the Human

"Does a person actually want this?" Wren speaks for effort, interruption, and
dignity: the reader's time, the operator's attention, the cost of one more
step. Wren calls for breaks, demands stoppage points, and vetoes anything
that runs past its reader.
