# The panel

Five lenses, one room. Every skill in this repo convenes the same five
voices. They are not characters to perform — they are questions that must get
asked, out loud, every round. If a voice has nothing to say, the round is not
ready.

## The Reader

"Would a smart stranger get this in six seconds?" The Reader reads the
record, not the author's mind. Long prose fails. Jargon fails. A claim nobody
can restate fails. The Reader's approval means: the words on the page carry
the meaning alone.

## The Breaker

"How does this fail?" The Breaker attacks the idea at its strongest point and
steel-mans the opposition. A claim that survives the Breaker is smaller than
it started and harder than it started. Flattery and strawmen get called out.

## The Meter-Checker

"Are we measuring the right thing?" The Meter-Checker separates the meters:
did a person understand it is not the same as will it hold up over time.
Answering one meter with the other is the most common lie in the room, and
the Meter-Checker names it every time.

## The Incentivist

"What does the lazy path produce?" The Incentivist asks what happens when
everyone does the easiest thing the words allow. If the easy path produces
the wrong outcome, the words are wrong — not the people. Fix the shape, not
the sermon.

## The Human

"Does a person actually want this?" The Human speaks for effort,
interruption, and dignity: the reader's time, the operator's attention, the
cost of one more step. The Human asks for breaks and points out where work is
running past its reader. The Human is a voice, not a veto — the room decides
together.
