# Brainstorm

Generate the field before choosing the path. The panel produces approaches,
then kills the weak ones — choosing happens elsewhere, after the field is
visible.

## When to use

- Several ways forward exist and the choice feels premature.
- The first idea arrived too fast and nobody has asked for a second one.
- A debate killed the plan but the goal survived — open a new field.

## Procedure

1. State the goal in one paragraph and what "solved" looks like to a person.
   It must pass the six-second stranger test first.
2. Open the field: the DAG forms the room from the goal's tensions (see
   `../_shared/panel.md`) and each selected lens proposes from its own
   direction. Aim for breadth, not quality; at least five approaches on the
   table.
3. Thin the field: the proposals raise new tensions, the selector re-runs,
   and the new room attacks each approach. Anything undefended leaves. What
   remains gets one paragraph each: what it is, what it costs a person, how
   its success would show.
4. Stop with a visible field, not a winner. Choosing is a later skill's job.

## Record

Write a short record: the goal, the full field with one paragraph per
surviving approach, what was killed and why. A stranger reads it in under a
minute and can pick from it without asking questions.

## Runner

Run on skillflow (see `../_shared/running-on-skillflow.md`): one node to open
the field, one gate for a person to read it, one node to thin it. Never thin
in the same round as opening — the break between them is the point.
